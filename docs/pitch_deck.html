07 / 10 · Competitive Analysis
Competitive analysis · Eckel

We don’t “do more.” We reject their assumption.

PlayerRational trade-off
FinanzguruBanks + contracts → aggregation habit
Finanzfluss CopilotContent + wealth → investor brand
Bank apps / LLMsLogin / chat — they will add AI

Choices are rational. Trajectory: assistants on dashboards.

Assumption we reject

“If young adults see money clearly enough, good decisions follow.”

Our view: Overview is solved by the bank app. The scarce product is a trustworthy decision in the moment.

Hard to fix on their path: dashboards are their identity. Our opening: decision layer on top.

Position against trajectory — not today’s logo grid.Kawasaki 07
