Problem · 3 / 10

They know they should.
They never get to it.

The tax return. Never filed, although most who file get money back.

The employer extras. Company pension subsidy, savings contributions: never claimed.

The pension. Everyone says: start early. Nobody shows your own gap or the first step.

The contracts. Subscriptions and insurance get more expensive, unnoticed.

It is not a lack of knowledge or apps. The bank app shows a balance, ChatGPT explains the pension. Nobody gets the postponed thing done with them.
95%

of 17 to 27-year-olds expect security from their retirement provision. 91% want it explained in plain language.

The wish is there. What is missing is the step from knowing to doing. Young employees with a few years in the job lose money every year because of it.

Solution · 4 / 10

Three questions. One agent.

1. Where do I stand? What is free this month, fixed costs, wealth, pension gap.

2. Where do I lose money? Open tax years, unclaimed employer extras, subscriptions, price increases. Each with an amount in euros.

3. What is my next step? One step, and Ahead takes it with you.

Validation · 5 / 10

Two tests before building. The buyers are the ones who put it off.

Test 1: the paid version at €49 a year with a bank file upload. 0 of 60 simulated buyers would pay. 56 named the upload, 52 wanted to see money first.

Test 2: a new offer, shown to five groups of twelve. Free check without a file, €29 a year only after Ahead has found €100 or more. 6 of 60 would pay.

What I learned: five of the six buyers gave the same reason: “I have put this off for years.” That defined my target group.

Business model & financials · 6 / 10

Free until Ahead finds you money. Then €29 a year.

Free: the check. Where you stand and what Ahead has found, with amounts. No account needed.

Ahead Plus, €29 a year: the doing. Ahead prepares the tax return, the letters and the applications with you, and checks every month. Offered only after a find of €100 or more.

Guarantee: less than €100 back in the first year and the fee is refunded.

What follows: winning a customer may cost at most €20. 5,000 customers bring €145k a year. Second source to test: employers pay for their young staff (50 employers × 200 people × €1.50 a month = €180k a year).

Market · 7 / 10

Start with employees aged 25 to 35.
Grow with them.

Who they are: a few years into the job, earning enough to care about security, never got around to it.

How many: 11.6 million employees never file a tax return. The share aged 25 to 35 is the number I still have to find.

How I reach them: employers who offer Ahead to their staff, and short videos that show what not filing costs.

Why they stay: in year one Ahead gets money back. From year two it tracks the pension gap and prepares the tax return every year.

Competition · 8 / 10

Others show the overview.
Ahead takes the next step.

Finanzguru: German finance app, connects 4,000+ banks, free with a paid plan at €0.99 a week. Strong on overview.

Taxfix: more than 10 million tax returns filed, from €39.99 each. Strong, but tax only.

ChatGPT: a quarter of Germans already ask an AI about money. It does not know their payments.

Honest gap: Ahead has no lasting advantage yet. Bank access and AI models can be rented by anyone. The advantage has to come from reaching this group first and from what Ahead learns with every case.
