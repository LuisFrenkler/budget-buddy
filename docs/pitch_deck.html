2 · Problem / Opportunity

They see money.
They still guess.

  • Bank apps: “What do I have?”
  • ChatGPT: no real ledger
  • Trips, moves, first salary: high stakes

Why now

95% of 16–29s bank online.

82% of Gen Z use the bank app — ~8% multibanking.

Chat is normal. Personal numbers are not.

Own the decision moment on top of the bank they already open.

7 · Competitive Analysis

We reject their assumption

Their trade-offs (rational)

Finanzguru → banks + contracts

Copilot → content + wealth

Banks / LLMs → login / chat. They will add AI.

Assumption we reject

“See money clearly → decide well.”

Our view: Overview is solved. The scarce product is a decision in the moment.

Hard to fix on their path: dashboards are their identity. Our opening: decision layer on the bank they already use.
