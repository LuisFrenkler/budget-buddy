Slide 4 · Solution

Buddy: ask before you spend

“Can I afford an €800 trip?” → answer from remaining budget, subscriptions, and goals.

  • Chat-first coach on the user’s ledger
  • Natural-language entry & receipt scan
  • Subscriptions + price-rise signals
  • What-if month simulator & next-step actions
  • Offline fallback · consent-gated cloud AI · no IBANs to models

Deliberately secondary

Bank link and portfolio are context for Buddy, not the hero product. That avoids a feature race against multibanking incumbents.

Orientation and calculation only — never regulated investment advice.

Slide 7 · Competition · assumption, not 2×2

We disagree with their assumption

Their rational trade-offs

Finanzguru — bank breadth + contracts → habit via aggregation.

Finanzfluss Copilot — content + wealth → trust via education.

Bank apps / LLMs — daily login / universal chat. They will add AI. That is the trajectory.

Assumption we reject

“If young adults see money clearly enough, good decisions follow.”

Our view: Overview is mostly solved by the bank app. The scarce product is a trustworthy decision in the moment with real numbers.

Why hard to fix on their path: identity, UX, and Plus packaging are built around dashboards. Bolting chat onto “show everything” still trains users to look — not to decide before spending. Our opening: decision layer on top of the bank they already use — not “full-stack multibanking.”
