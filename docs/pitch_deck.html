07 · Competitive Analysis

Why can’t they just copy you?

They optimize for overview.
We optimize for the decision.

Their rational bets

Finanzguru — banks + contracts

Finanzfluss Copilot — content + wealth

Banks / LLMs — login / chat (+ AI soon)

Trajectory: assistants bolted onto dashboards.

Assumption we reject

“See money clearly → decide well.”

Overview is solved by the bank app. The scarce product is a trustworthy decision in the moment.

Hard to fix: their identity is aggregation. Our opening: decision layer on top.
