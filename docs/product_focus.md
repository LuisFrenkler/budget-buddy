# Ahead — Product Focus (Life Start)

**Category:** Coach for first own finances in Germany (~18–27)  
**Job:** One next step today, from their numbers — not overview, not ChatGPT without a ledger.

## North star

Share of Buddy sessions that are **life-start / affordability** questions  
(salary, rent, Abos, trip, WG, Notgroschen, BAV) — not depot/wealth vanity.

## Ship (core)

1. Buddy + next step from ledger  
2. Affordability / noch frei / what-if  
3. Abos + price rises  
4. Quick entry + receipt  
5. WG / splits  
6. Notgroschen + first BAV question (orientation)  
7. Local-first, consent, offline Buddy  

## Playbooks (must become real flows)

| Playbook | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Erste Abrechnung** | Netto + Fixkosten + „noch frei“ + Notgroschen-Start |
| **Erste Wohnung / Umzug** | Miete/Kaution/Einrichtung tragbar? |
| **Abos & Löcher** | Was läuft, was teurer wurde, was killen |

Until these exist as guided flows, the niche is only positioning.

## Kill / demote (do not market as hero)

- Depot charts as store/landing hero  
- „2.500 Banken“ as main promise  
- Nettovermögen / wealth distribution as first Buddy chips  
- Feature-parity with Finanzguru or Finanzfluss  

Depot and bank stay **optional context** for Buddy. Never the story.

## Messaging test

If someone says “Isn’t this Finanzguru + ChatGPT?”:

> No. Those show everything or talk without your ledger. Ahead is for people in their first money years who need the next step before they spend.
